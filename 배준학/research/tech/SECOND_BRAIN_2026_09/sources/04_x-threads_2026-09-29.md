### Top 15, ranked by relevance to Luffi × how much buzz it got

1. **OpenAI "Dreaming" (Dreaming V3) for ChatGPT memory.** Published Jun 4, 2026: https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-memory-dreaming/
   - **What it does:** a background process consolidates years of chats into memory and updates facts as time passes (for example, "going to Singapore in July" becomes "went to Singapore in July 2026"). Users get a Memory Summary page.
   - **Numbers:** factual recall of 82.8% is **[UNVERIFIED]**. Secondary reports disagree on the baseline (41.5% vs 67.9%), and the primary page shows these only as charts.
   - **Critique:** Shlok Khemani (Jun 6, https://www.shloked.com/chatgpt-memory-2026) points out that the summary is only a view for users. It is not what gets injected, and edits don't change the underlying memory.
   - **Mem0 explainer on X (Jul 1):** https://x.com/mem0ai/article/2071990201531118063
   - **For Luffi:** your model vendor now ships consolidation. Your edge is an inspectable, editable graph with provenance, which is exactly where OpenAI is being criticized.

2. **Pool, a screenshot memory app.** Launch post Jun 12: https://x.com/pooool/status/2065097298946777226
   - **Engagement:** about 1.72M views, 1.5K likes, 1.3K bookmarks, 217 replies.
   - **Funding:** over $2M pre-seed from General Catalyst and Kima (TechCrunch Jun 11: https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/11/pools-new-app-turns-your-screenshots-into-a-searchable-memory-bank/).
   - **Claim:** "the context of the future is your camera roll." It sorts screenshots into "pools", recovers the original links, and lets screenshots fade by relevance. iOS only, free.
   - **For Luffi:** the most direct competitor to your capture step. Luffi has to win on goals, tasks and the feedback loop, not on organizing.

3. **The OS vendors are absorbing "screen → action".**
   - **Siri AI (WWDC, Jun 8):** on-screen awareness plus personal context, built on Gemini-based Apple Foundation Models (https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/09/wwdc-2026-everything-announced-on-siri-ai-os-27-apple-intelligence-and-more/).
   - **Pixel 11 (Aug 12):** Magic Cue becomes Gemini "Proactive Assistance".
   - **Samsung S26 "Now Nudge":** runs on-device and brings back a screenshot, such as a gate code or flight number, when the context matches.
   - **Nothing Essential Space:** added a "For You" tab of action items and automatic event detection (Mar 2026).
   - **For Luffi:** Samsung matters most in Korea. One-off nudges are becoming commodity, so the moat has to come from linking many captures over time.

4. **Anthropic "dreaming" for Claude Managed Agents.** Research preview announced around May 6; the blog's date reads May 19 (https://claude.com/blog/new-in-claude-managed-agents).
   - A scheduled job reviews sessions and memory and restructures memory. You can choose to **review changes before they land**.
   - Harvey reports about 6x higher completion rates.
   - The earlier memory public beta post (Apr 24) had 655K views and 9K likes: https://x.com/claudeai/status/2047421844311949513
   - **For Luffi:** the model to copy is "propose a diff, then apply it" for re-linking.

5. **GBrain (Garry Tan).** https://github.com/garrytan/gbrain — 30.4k stars, MIT, TypeScript, last pushed 2026-09-29.
   - Markdown in git is the source of truth, indexed into Postgres.
   - It builds a typed graph by pattern matching, with **zero LLM calls**.
   - Facts carry their sources and support correction and withdrawal. Answers include a note on what the brain doesn't know yet, and a dream cycle runs overnight.
   - On its own BrainBench refresh (Sep 9), the graph adapter scores R@5 0.979 vs 0.687 for plain hybrid search. That is a vendor-run eval.
   - Mem0 explainer (Jun 26): https://x.com/mem0ai/article/2070541048527609885
   - **For Luffi:** the closest match to your constraints (Postgres, provenance, cheap deterministic links).

6. **Agent Memory Leaderboard (AML).**
   - **Links:** @AgentMemoryL; https://agentmemoryleaderboard.ai; repo https://github.com/AML-memory/agent-memory-leaderboard (1.2k stars, no license).
   - **Timeline:** launched Jul 29 by 25+ institutions. Cycle 1 results came out Aug 12. Cycle 2 opened Sep 20, closes Oct 31, and results are due mid-November.
   - **Method:** the answer model is fixed; each system exposes only Add and Search.
   - **Commercial textual track:** MemoraX 58.02 (a roughly 6-month-old company per joto.ai **[UNVERIFIED]**), MemOS 45.89, Cognee 42.61, TencentDB 41.48, Mem0 41.40, MemPalace 39.48, Hindsight Cloud 38.54, Supermemory 29.63.
   - **Open-source track:** the top entry, InvMem, scores 45.06. A plain **SQLite full-text search baseline scores 41.79**, level with Mem0 at 42.07.
   - **Temporal & Event Reasoning** sits around 16–24 for most systems.
   - Zep/Graphiti and Letta are not on the board.
   - **For Luffi:** the first controlled comparison. It says fancy memory barely beats full-text search and that temporal reasoning is still unsolved.

7. **Hermes Agent (Nous Research).** https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent — 249.9k stars, MIT, Python, pushed today.
   - **Memory:** a MEMORY.md of about 2,200 characters and a USER.md of about 1,375 characters, loaded as a frozen snapshot. Past sessions are searchable with SQLite full-text search.
   - **Plug-in providers:** Honcho, OpenViking, Mem0, Holographic, RetainDB, ByteRover and Supermemory.
   - Skills are created automatically, and memory and skill writes go through an approval step.
   - **For Luffi:** a small always-loaded profile plus a searchable log is now the default baseline to beat.

8. **OpenClaw Dreaming.** https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw — 390.8k stars. Dreaming reached general availability in April.
   - Three phases (light, REM, deep), and a DREAMS.md diary for human review.
   - A fact is promoted only past score ≥0.8, recalled ≥3 times, across ≥3 distinct queries **[UNVERIFIED; from third-party guides]**.
   - **For Luffi:** a concrete rule to reuse. Promote a capture to "interest" or "goal" only after repeated signals.

9. **Coding-agent memory plugins.**
   - **claude-mem:** 94.9k stars, Apache-2.0.
   - **agentmemory (rohitg00):** 29.0k stars, Apache-2.0.
   - Both capture tool use, compress it with an LLM, and inject it back. Developers Digest (May 16): "Useful Only If You Audit What It Remembers."
   - **For Luffi:** medium. They show the capture → compress → inject loop working at scale, but in a different domain.

10. **Quarq Agent.**
    - Harrison Chase quote-tweeted it on May 27 (76K views, 382 likes): https://x.com/hwchase17/status/2059487107144655356

11. **Bosun (Hanno Labs).** Show HN around late June, 2 points: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48493954 ; model https://huggingface.co/Hanno-Labs/bosun-xs (Apache-2.0).
    - A LoRA of Qwen3-Reranker at 0.6B/4B that scores whether an edge is "supported, non-redundant, still-true" against a rule you write in plain language.
    - **For Luffi:** high relevance and almost no buzz. A cheap way to enforce "a link is not evidence of an action" without a GPT call per edge.

12. **"Memory engineering" discourse.**
    - Richmond Alake (Sep 16, 160 views) distinguishes it from context engineering: https://x.com/richmondalake/status/2100148660537364512
    - Oracle AI Agent Memory (Jul 14) puts working, semantic, episodic and procedural memory in one database with auditing.
    - Paul Iusztin's post "5 mistakes" (HN, https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48337689):
      - Entity resolution is not the same as dedup.
      - His thresholds: similarity ≥0.95 auto-merges, 0.85–0.95 goes to review, below 0.85 becomes a new node.
      - You also need "reasoning memory".
    - **For Luffi:** keep the review band, but turn his auto-merge into a *suggested* merge so nothing merges silently.

13. **Benchmark fights.**
    - **MemPalace:** 59.3k stars, MIT. It claimed 100% on LongMemEval, later 96.6%, and was widely debunked as "a ChromaDB benchmark". Allegations of bought stars are **[UNVERIFIED]**. It scored 39.48 on AML.
    - **Bench'd leaderboard (DEV, Aug 29):** a GPT-4o-mini with no memory at all placed third.
    - **LLMS3 (Jul 26):** Mem0 reports 92.5% on LoCoMo; an independent study found 77–81%.
    - **ForgetEval (arXiv 2606.15903, single author):** failures in production are mostly *forgetting*, not recall. The claim that Graphiti scores 4.4–7.0% on adversarial deletion is **[UNVERIFIED, secondary source]**.

14. **Proactive agents.**
    - **ChatGPT Pulse was retired on Jun 17** and folded into scheduled tasks.
    - **Cognition acquired Poke** (Jul 24, "low nine figures"). Poke had over 100M messages in 3 months and was the first AI agent approved for Apple Messages for Business.
    - **Poppy** (May 13, $1.25M pre-seed) pulls signals from calendar, mail, Health and iMessage.
    - **For Luffi:** a standalone feed didn't keep users, while presence in a conversation channel and personality did.

15. **Caution on self-improving agents.**
    - **PAST-Bench** (Aug 4, arXiv 2608.04003, built "Hermes+"): gains from retained experience are inconsistent. They are strongest when the agent is *replacing outdated state*.
    - **Skill misevolution** (Aug 13, arXiv 2608.12851): all 21 evolved configurations produced unsafe artifacts; the authors propose a SafeEvolve wrapper.
    - **For Luffi:** let feedback update memory and state, not rewrite the procedures unsupervised.

### Hype vs substance

- **Hype:**
  - Vendor LoCoMo and LongMemEval scores in the 90s (Quarq 98.2%, MemPalace, several others) that nobody has reproduced.
  - Leaderboards with broken aggregation (Bench'd).
  - Star counts as a proxy for quality (MemPalace).
  - "The industry declared memory solved" (Moorcheh.ai).
- **Substance:**
  - AML's controlled runs.
  - OpenAI and Anthropic shipping consolidation to hundreds of millions of users.
  - The open, heavily used runtimes (Hermes, OpenClaw, GBrain) all converging on the same pattern: bounded markdown, full-text search, scheduled consolidation.
  - Poke's usage metrics.
- **Mixed:** temporal knowledge graphs. AML temporal scores are low almost everywhere except MemoraX and MemOS, and Zep/Graphiti hasn't been evaluated there, so there's no controlled evidence either way.

### Takeaways

1. **Background consolidation is now expected, not a differentiator.** OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenClaw, GBrain and Honcho all "dream". What still separates products is whether changes can be reviewed: diffs, diaries, withdrawal. For Luffi, this means two paths:
   - A fast write path: capture → immutable evidence plus provisional facts, within seconds.
   - A scheduled consolidation job that *proposes* merges and re-links for the user to approve.
2. **Simple baselines are hard to beat.** Full-text search roughly matches Mem0 on AML, GBrain links without LLM calls, and Hermes uses about 3.5K characters of profile. Start with Postgres full-text search plus pgvector and deterministic linking. Spend GPT calls on extraction and suggestions.
3. **Provenance, correction and forgetting are the open ground.** GBrain's withdrawal, Eywa, Bosun, the critique of OpenAI's summary page, and ForgetEval all point here. Memory governance and deletion are the weakest measured capabilities, and they line up with Luffi's rules.
4. **Screenshot → action is being commoditized** by Apple, Google, Samsung, Nothing and Pool. Luffi's defensible layer is longitudinal: goals, choices and progress across many captures.
5. **Proactivity should follow the user's own goals and happen inside the flow.** Pulse was retired while Poke got acquired. Run self-improvement at the context and memory layer with guardrails, not as self-rewriting skills.
